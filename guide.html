核心設計：反應區與現場問卷The core idea: zones and in-situ surveys

研究者在地圖上畫定反應區。受測者走進去的那一刻，手機擷取當下的座標、心率與環境快照，並立即跳出現場問卷。若人走出區外而問卷還沒填完，那份作答自動作廢——這是刻意的：生態瞬時評估（EMA）的效度就建立在「答案確實來自那個當下、那個地點」，事後補填會毀掉它。

The researcher draws response zones on a map. The moment a participant walks into one, the phone captures the current coordinates, heart rate and an environmental snapshot, and immediately presents the in-situ survey. If they leave the zone before finishing, that response is discarded automatically. This is deliberate: the validity of ecological momentary assessment rests on the answer genuinely coming from that moment and that place, and letting people fill it in afterwards would destroy it.

二、研究者：開案四站2 · Researcher: the four stations

從專案列表建立專案，點入後依序走過四站：1 設定 → 2 範圍 → 3 問卷 → 4 資料。四站是作業順序，不是選單——問卷與範圍沒設好，受測者端就沒有東西可跑。

Create a project from the project list, then work through four stations in order: 1 Settings → 2 Zones → 3 Survey → 4 Data. They are a sequence, not a menu: until zones and surveys are set, there is nothing for the participant side to run.

專案代號建立後不可更改。它是所有資料歸檔與跨檔合併的欄位鍵。請用英數短碼（可含 - 與 _），例如 park2026。名稱與備註隨時可改，代號不行。

The project code cannot be changed once created. It is the key under which everything is filed and by which files are merged. Use a short alphanumeric code (- and _ allowed), e.g. park2026. Name and note stay editable; the code does not.

1 設定 · 名稱與狀態1 Settings · name and status

填名稱與備註。狀態分兩種：進行中＝手機端可以選到這個專案；封存＝手機端不再顯示。收案結束就改成封存，受測者才不會誤選。

Set the name and note. Two states: Active means the project is selectable on the phone; Archived means it no longer appears there. Archive a project when collection ends so participants cannot pick it by mistake.

「自清單移除」只移除登記，讓專案不再出現在清單與手機端。已儲存的範圍、問卷與量測資料都留在後端，以相同代號重新建立即可復原。

“Remove from list” only removes the registration, so the project disappears from the list and from the phone. Saved zones, surveys and measurements stay on the backend; recreate the project with the same code to get them back.

2 範圍 · 畫反應區2 Zones · draw response zones

按「新增區域」，在地圖上依序點出頂點（至少 3 點），按「完成多邊形」，最後按「儲存反應區」。可畫多個不規則區域，畫錯用「復原一點」或「取消」。

Click “Add zone”, tap the vertices on the map in order (at least 3), click “Close polygon”, and finally click “Save zones”. You can draw several irregular zones; use “Undo point” or “Cancel” to correct mistakes.

受測者端不需要任何設定：手機選定專案後會自動取用這裡存的範圍。

The participant side needs no configuration: once the phone selects the project, it picks these zones up automatically.

3 問卷 · 兩份，觸發方式在此設定3 Survey · two of them, plus the trigger

每個專案有兩份問卷。入組問卷：受測者第一次使用時填一次（基本資料、email），可一鍵套用範本。現場問卷（EMA）：每次觸發都填，可上傳現成問卷（PDF／Word .docx／純文字）由 AI 解析成題目，也可套用五題範本。

Each project has two surveys. The intake survey is filled once, the first time a participant uses the app (background details, email); a template applies it in one click. The in-situ survey (EMA) is filled on every trigger; you can upload an existing questionnaire (PDF, Word .docx or plain text) for AI to parse into items, or apply a five-item template.

觸發方式有三種：範圍（走進反應區）、定時（每 N 分鐘一次）、或兩者。量尺題的最小／最大值一次套用到全部量尺題。可勾選支援語言並指定預設語言，手機依系統語言顯示，答案落在同一欄位，跨國收案也能直接合併。

Three trigger modes: Zone (on entering a response zone), Timed (every N minutes), or Both. The scale minimum and maximum apply to every scale item at once. Tick the supported languages and pick a default; the phone shows the participant's system language, while answers land in the same columns, so multi-country collection merges directly.

AI 譯文是草稿。標準化量表正式收案請以官方譯本覆核。掃描影像的 PDF 抽不出文字，要先 OCR；舊版 .doc 請先另存為 .docx。

AI translations are drafts. For standardized instruments in real collection, check them against the official translation. Scanned image PDFs contain no extractable text and need OCR first; save legacy .doc as .docx.

三、受測者：一次走測3 · Participant: one walk

受測者自己操作手機，研究者不必在場遙控。流程如下。

Participants operate their own phone; no researcher needs to be present to drive it. The flow is as follows.

在手機上選定專案Select the project on the phone

HealthProbe 會列出進行中的專案。選定後，該專案的反應區與問卷自動下載，不需手動填任何網址或編號。

HealthProbe lists the active projects. Once one is selected, its zones and surveys download automatically — no URLs or codes to type in.

填一次入組問卷Fill the intake survey once

第一次使用該專案時填，含 email。之後同一專案不再重複問。

Filled the first time they use that project, including their email. It is not asked again for the same project.

開始走測Start the walk

手機開始記錄軌跡、環境與心率。手錶戴著就好，不必自己開體能訓練，也不必做任何設定——心率由手機統一操控。第一次會要求定位權限，請選「永遠允許」，背景記錄需要它。

The phone starts recording the track, the environment and heart rate. Just wear the watch — there is no workout to start and nothing to configure; the phone drives heart-rate collection. Location permission is requested the first time: choose “Always Allow”, which background recording requires.

走進反應區 → 當場作答Enter a zone → answer on the spot

走進區內時問卷會自己跳出來，當場填完。走出區外而還沒填完的會自動作廢，這是設計如此，不是錯誤。若觸發方式設為定時，則每隔設定的分鐘數也會跳一次。

The survey appears by itself on entering a zone; finish it on the spot. Leaving the zone unfinished discards that response — by design, not a fault. If the trigger is set to timed, it also appears at the configured interval.

結束走測並上傳End the walk and upload

走完就結束本場，資料整包送到後端。心率若還沒同步完，先上傳一次即可，之後再上傳一次補齊（見第五節）。

End the session when the walk is done and the whole bundle goes to the backend. If heart rate has not finished syncing, upload once now and upload again later to fill it in (see section 5).

忘記按結束的防護。App 在走測滿 3 小時會提醒，滿 6 小時自動結束並上傳。即使如此，仍請養成走完就結束的習慣——超過 12 小時的場次混雜了睡眠與通勤，主控台會標橘色警告且不建議分析。

Protection against forgetting to stop. The app reminds you at 3 hours and automatically ends and uploads at 6 hours. Even so, make ending the session a habit: a session longer than 12 hours mixes in sleep and commuting, and the console flags it orange and advises against analysing it.

四、走測中的即時監看4 · Live monitoring during a walk

即時監看把走測中的裝置畫在地圖上。走測中的手機每半分鐘回報一次位置與最近心率；超過三分鐘沒回報就視為離線、不再顯示。可以只看單一專案，也可以看全部專案。

The live monitor plots active devices on a map. A phone on a walk reports its position and latest heart rate every 30 seconds; if nothing arrives for over three minutes it is treated as offline and hidden. You can scope the view to one project or watch them all.

監看畫面的心率不是即時值。心率來自 Apple Watch 經 HealthKit 遞送，可能落後實際數十秒到數分鐘。它適合用來確認「這台裝置還在收、人在這條路上」，不適合用來做即時生理判斷。

The heart rate on this screen is not live. It reaches the phone from the Apple Watch via HealthKit and can lag reality by tens of seconds to several minutes. Use it to confirm that a device is still collecting and where the person is — not to make real-time physiological judgements.

五、心率為什麼會晚到5 · Why heart rate arrives late

Apple Watch 量到的健康資料，是有空才同步到 iPhone，不是即時的。放上充電座時手錶會跑背景同步，所以最穩、最快。不充電資料還是會到，只是慢且不固定，可能幾分鐘也可能幾十分鐘。

Health data measured by the Apple Watch syncs to the iPhone when it gets the chance, not in real time. Putting the watch on its charger triggers a background sync, which is the fastest and most reliable route. Without charging the data still arrives, just slowly and unpredictably — minutes, sometimes tens of minutes.

正式收案的實務做法：不要求受測者走完馬上充電，那不切實際。改成走完先上傳一次，等資料陸續同步進來之後再上傳一次。同一場次重複上傳會覆蓋更新，不會產生重複場次，所以補齊很安全。研究者在主控台看到某場次心率筆數偏少時，請受測者再上傳一次即可。

In practice, for real collection: do not ask participants to charge the watch the moment they finish — that is unrealistic. Have them upload once when the walk ends, then upload again after the data has had time to sync. Re-uploading the same session overwrites it rather than creating a duplicate, so filling in gaps is safe. When the console shows few physiology records for a session, just ask the participant to upload again.

六、資料主控台與匯出6 · The data console and exports

主控台開頁就自動抓回本專案所有已上傳場次，依時間把心率與軌跡對接成帶 GPS 的資料點，全員疊在同一張地圖上。每位參與者是一張卡片（編號 × 場次）。

The console automatically pulls back every uploaded session for the project, joins heart rate to the track by time into GPS-bearing points, and overlays all participants on one map. Each participant appears as a card (code × session).

控制項Control 作用What it does
地圖上色方式Map colouring 依編號＝每位一色，看誰走了哪裡；依數值＝選一個指標（如心率）依冷暖著色，看沿路高低。By code gives each participant a colour, showing who walked where. By value colours points cool-to-warm by a chosen metric (e.g. heart rate), showing where it ran high or low.
時間容差Time tolerance
90 s		 心率與軌跡各自取樣的時刻不同，兩者時間差在容差內才配成同一點。放寬會得到較多點但配對較鬆。Heart rate and track are sampled at different instants; the two are paired into one point only if they fall within this tolerance. Loosening it yields more points but looser matches.
GPS 精度上限GPS accuracy limit
50 m		 精度差於此值的軌跡點不採用。室內與高樓間 GPS 會飄，這道門檔掉不可信的位置。Track points less accurate than this are dropped. GPS drifts indoors and between tall buildings; this threshold discards untrustworthy positions.
資料點大小Point size 純顯示用，不影響計算。Display only; it does not affect any computation.

匯出與封存Export and archiving

產出專案 Excel：每人一分頁。存到 Google Drive：也可手動觸發，但場次上傳後系統本來就會每 5 分鐘自動封存一次，按專案代號與場次分資料夾。

Export project Excel: one sheet per participant. Save to Google Drive: available manually, though sessions are archived automatically every 5 minutes anyway, foldered by project code and session.

Excel 的欄位名固定中英並陳（例如 氣溫 AirTemp (°C)），不隨介面語言改變。這是刻意的：跨檔、跨國合併時，欄位鍵必須唯一，不能因為匯出時介面是英文就變成另一個名字。

Excel column names are always bilingual (e.g. 氣溫 AirTemp (°C)) and do not follow the interface language. This is deliberate: when merging files across sessions and countries the column key must be unique, and must not change just because the interface happened to be in English at export time.

主控台另有「進階：本機離線模式」，展開後可把 HealthProbe 匯出的 JSON 直接拖入卡片，資料只在本機處理。那是後端抓不到時的備援，正常流程不需要用到。

The console also has an “Advanced: local offline mode”. Expanding it lets you drag HealthProbe's exported JSON straight onto a card, processed entirely in the browser. It is a fallback for when the backend cannot be reached, not part of the normal flow.

七、分析結果怎麼讀7 · How to read the analysis

主控台的脈絡分析與點層級分析不是普通圖表，背後有一串刻意的方法決定。不理解它們，很容易把假相關當成發現。

The context and point-level analyses in the console are not ordinary charts; a series of deliberate methodological decisions sits behind them. Without understanding those, it is easy to mistake a spurious correlation for a finding.

分析在單一場次之內進行Analysis stays within a single session

跨場次把資料點混在一起，會把場次之間的差異偷渡回來，可能產生辛普森悖論——各場次內部的關係明明一致，混起來卻反向。場次是分層，不是分析單位。跨場次的環境效果應以配對設計檢驗：同一條路線、相近時段、不同天氣各走數次，比較配對差值。

Pooling points across sessions smuggles between-session differences back in and can produce Simpson's paradox: the relationship is consistent within every session yet reverses when they are combined. Sessions are a stratum, not the unit of analysis. Test environmental effects across sessions with a paired design instead: walk the same route at comparable times of day under different weather several times, and compare the paired differences.

必須控制速度與晝夜Speed and time of day must be controlled

活動強度是心率最主要的決定因子，不先控掉它，天氣的效果無從談起。而時刻是環形變數：23 時與 1 時相鄰，用線性的「小時」控制不掉晝夜節律，必須拆成 sin／cos 兩個分量，以多元迴歸殘差化處理。

Activity intensity is the dominant determinant of heart rate; until it is controlled there is no point discussing weather. Time of day is a circular variable: 23:00 and 01:00 are adjacent, so a linear “hour” term cannot remove the diurnal rhythm. It must be decomposed into sin and cos components and residualized by multiple regression.

這不是理論顧慮。實測某筆跨日資料，氣溫與心率的相關 r=0.88；只控速度剩 0.87，再加線性小時剩 0.86，改成環形分量後掉到 0.02。那個 0.88 完全是晝夜節律假裝出來的。

This is not a theoretical worry. In one real multi-day dataset, air temperature correlated with heart rate at r=0.88. Controlling speed left 0.87; adding a linear hour term left 0.86; switching to circular components dropped it to 0.02. That 0.88 was the diurnal rhythm in disguise.

自相關分三級處理Autocorrelation is handled at three levels

取樣間隔並不均勻（運動中約 5 秒一筆，靜止時稀疏），所以不能用「相鄰兩筆」定義自相關，而是用實際時間戳估出去相關時間 τ。三級結果並列呈現：

Sampling is not evenly spaced (roughly every 5 seconds while moving, sparse while still), so autocorrelation cannot be defined on “adjacent records”. The decorrelation time τ is estimated from the actual timestamps instead, and three levels are reported side by side:

  1. 只報 r（效果量）。永遠成立，不受自相關影響。Report r alone (effect size). Always valid, unaffected by autocorrelation.
  2. 以 n_eff = 總時長 ÷ 2τ 當有效樣本數，據此算 p。Take n_eff = total duration ÷ 2τ as the effective sample size and compute p from it.
  3. 切成長於 2τ 的區塊，各自求 r，以區塊間的變異給 95% 信賴區間。Cut the series into blocks longer than 2τ, compute r in each, and derive a 95% CI from the between-block variation.

三者以區塊結果最值得採信。兩個 p 值不一致時，以區塊為準。

Of the three, trust the block result most. When the two p-values disagree, go with the blocks.

移動模式與時間加權Movement mode and time weighting

步行、騎行、車輛、靜止、室內的心率—速度關係截然不同，所以分析要分層。目前的模式判別是推定值，依速度、心率抬升與 GPS 精度推算；App 已加入 CoreMotion 實測，等實測資料回來就改用實測值。

The heart-rate/speed relationship differs sharply between walking, cycling, vehicle travel, standing still and being indoors, so the analysis stratifies by mode. Mode is currently inferred from speed, heart-rate elevation and GPS accuracy; the app now records CoreMotion's measured activity type, and the console will switch to the measured value once such data comes back.

模式分布一律以時間加權呈現，不是點數。實測一筆騎行 30 分鐘、靜止 10 小時的資料：點數上騎行佔 85%，時間上只佔 9%。按點數看會嚴重高估密集取樣的那段。

Mode distribution is always shown weighted by time, never by point count. In one real dataset with 30 minutes of cycling and 10 hours of being still, cycling was 85% of the points but only 9% of the time. Counting points badly overstates whichever stretch was sampled densely.

超過 12 小時的場次不可分析，主控台會顯示橘色警告。那通常是走測沒按結束、App 繼續記錄，資料裡混了睡眠與通勤，任何相關都不具意義。

Sessions longer than 12 hours must not be analysed; the console shows an orange warning. They usually mean the walk was never ended and the app kept recording, so sleep and commuting are mixed in and no correlation means anything.

八、常見狀況8 · Troubleshooting

狀況Symptom 原因與處理Cause and fix
某場次心率筆數很少Few physiology records in a session 手錶還沒把資料同步給手機。請受測者稍後再上傳一次，同場次會覆蓋更新。放上充電座可加快同步。The watch has not synced to the phone yet. Ask the participant to upload again later; the same session is overwritten. Putting the watch on the charger speeds the sync up.

即時監看看不到裝置A device is missing from the live monitor 超過三分鐘沒回報就視為離線。可能走測已結束、手機沒網路，或不在這個專案底下。Anything silent for over three minutes counts as offline. The walk may have ended, the phone may have no network, or the device may be under a different project.
場次時長超過 12 小時A session is longer than 12 hours 忘了按結束。該場次不可分析，見第七節。App 在 3 小時會提醒、6 小時自動結束。The walk was never ended. That session cannot be analysed — see section 7. The app reminds at 3 hours and auto-ends at 6.

九、資料格式（給接手者）9 · Data formats (for maintainers)

一個場次在後端是一筆記錄，key 為 專案代號/編號/場次。六條流包在同一筆裡：

A session is one record on the backend, keyed project/code/session. All six streams live inside it:

physiologylocationenvironment emaactivityaltitude

{ project, code, session, uploadedAt, final, counts, physiology[], location[], environment[], ema[], activity[], altitude[], intake }

後三條流（ema／activity／altitude）向下相容：舊版 App 未送時為空陣列、計數為 0，上傳不會失敗。final:false 代表走測中的定期備份，Drive 封存要等定稿。

The last three streams (ema, activity, altitude) are backward compatible: when an older app does not send them they are empty arrays with a count of 0, and the upload still succeeds. final:false marks a periodic backup taken mid-walk; Drive archiving waits for the final version.

流Stream 主控台讀取的欄位Fields the console reads
physiology { metricType, time, value, unit, sourceDevice }
metricType 如 heart_rate、spo2 等metricType e.g. heart_rate, spo2
location { time, lat, lng, accuracyM }
lon 亦可。App 另送 speedMps／courseDeg／altitudeM 等 CoreLocation 原生值，主控台目前尚未讀用。lon is also accepted. The app additionally sends CoreLocation native values such as speedMps, courseDeg and altitudeM, which the console does not yet consume.
environment { time, snapshot{ airTempC, apparentTempC, humidityPct, usAqi, pm25, pm10, windSpeedMs, weatherDesc, … } }

Drive 封存的檔名（空流略過）：問卷.json、生理.json、軌跡.json、環境.json、活動類型.json、高度.json。同名檔補傳時原地更新，不會產生重複。

File names written to Drive (empty streams are skipped): 問卷.json, 生理.json, 軌跡.json, 環境.json, 活動類型.json, 高度.json. On re-upload, existing files are updated in place rather than duplicated.
